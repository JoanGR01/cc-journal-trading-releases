# CC Journal — Trading edition

A trading journal for Windows: log your trades, see your stats by day and by
account, follow the market, and screen stocks against a set of value-investing
principles.

## Download

**[Download CC Journal Trading](https://github.com/JoanGR01/cc-journal-trading-releases/releases/latest/download/CC-Journal-Trading-Setup.exe)**

That link always gives you the newest version. Install it over the old one —
your journal is kept.

Windows may warn you about an unknown publisher: choose **More info → Run
anyway**.

## What is inside

- **Dashboard, Trades, Calendar, Accounts** — your journal: every trade with
  its emotion log and chart screenshot, profit and loss by day, and your prop
  accounts with their payout rules.
- **Realtime Info** — the market's volatility, live.
- **VI Screener** — a stock judged against seven value-investing principles,
  plus a dropdown of ten stocks that meet them today.
- **Calculator** and **Data** — a calculator that keeps its sum across screens,
  and backups, exports and database tools.

Everything stays on your own computer. There is no account, no cloud and no
sign-in.

## Updating

There is no automatic update. When a new version is announced, download from
the link above and install over what you have.
